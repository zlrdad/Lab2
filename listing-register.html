Inclusion criteria & how to read this table
Core (87) = the Globe and Mail $100M Club, Dec 2025 (77 private companies with US$100M+ revenue — single-sourced to the Globe's investigation) ∪ the vault-verified unicorn roster on the outer-bound basis (27, incl. two legacy-⚠ marks: Dapper Labs, eSentire). Annex (18) = the Globe's "trending toward US$100M" names + the vault deep-tech watch — visible pipeline, excluded from every headline stat. Public companies, departed companies (foreign-HQ) and acquired names are excluded by construction. Revenue floors: best-known figure, else the bracket floor — the US$15.4B total is a conservative minimum. Valuations: last priced mark only, dated, with a confidence grade; blank = no priced mark exists (never inferred); the US$77.5B all-marks sum mixes 2020–2026 vintages and is not a portfolio value. Ownership is asserted only where the vault's July-2026 refresh sourced it. Currency: assessed in USD, CAD dual-displayed at ~US$1 = C$1.44. Full methodology: the register's anchor page and dataset in the vault (Data/ + Sources/, July 2026); source layer: the Canadian High-Growth Company Tracker deep-dive (July 29, 2026).
Why 27 unicorns, not the widely-cited 33
Tracxn's 33 (Tier-8 aggregator) carries names that fail verification: Nexii (creditor protection) · LeddarTech (bankrupt) · SSENSE (insolvency resale ~US$78M) · Clearco & Paper (collapsed) · Tenstorrent (HQ to Santa Clara, 2023) · Xanadu (went public Mar 2026 — a listing success) · Nuvei (Advent take-private) · Maropost & Hootsuite (never priced at US$1B) · Axelar (not Canada-HQ). The same verification added capital-efficient names aggregators miss: StackAdapt, Fullscript, Jane Software, GeoComply, Svante, Semios, Nord Quantique, Nesto. Per-name paragraphs live in the register workbook's "Gap vs Tracxn 33" sheet.
Known limitations
Tech-scoped by construction — large private non-tech candidates (industrial, consumer, food) await a phase-2 expansion on the Financial Post largest-private-companies spine. The Globe roster is single-sourced. Most ownership fields are unverified (asserted tags only where the vault refresh sourced them). "Listing potential" ≠ listing intent — see the IPO-signal column. Portless and Turbopuffer HQs unverified. CB Insights links resolved for 103 of 105 (SSENSE, Redbrick have no profile); ~15 live under former-name slugs, each page-verified.
